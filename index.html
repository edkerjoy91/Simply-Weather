ABOUT THE CONSENSUS

The displayed temperature forecast is the mean of the available deterministic models. Temperature confidence is based on model spread. Precipitation probability is calculated separately from independent ensemble systems including DWD ICON‑EPS, NOAA GEFS, CMC GEPS and BOM ACCESS‑GE. Each probability is the share of ensemble members forecasting more than 0.1 mm in the hour, and the app shows their consensus rather than treating deterministic model agreement as probability.
